How to Choose Electrical Test Equipment

A beginner's guide to choosing basic electrical measurement equipment safely and sensibly.

Key specifications

For a multimeter or other test instrument, consider measurement categories, maximum voltage and current, input protection, accuracy and the type of work you will perform.

Safety

Never assume an instrument is safe because it is inexpensive or has many functions. Use equipment within its rated category and follow laboratory and manufacturer instructions.
