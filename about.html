ABOUT US

Helping students make more informed purchases.

CampusKit Guide is an independent product-research website focused on practical student needs.

What we cover

Our guides focus on college essentials, electrical engineering tools, tech and study accessories, hostel organization and everyday personal care. We intentionally keep the scope connected to student life rather than trying to recommend everything on the internet.

How we approach recommendations

We start with the problem a product category solves. Then we explain the specifications, compatibility questions, trade-offs and practical considerations a reader should check. We do not claim that one product is perfect for everyone.

Prices, availability, specifications, seller information and customer feedback can change. Readers should verify current information on the retailer's product page before purchasing.

Our editorial principles

  • Explain the category before encouraging a purchase.
  • Prefer useful specifications over marketing language.
  • Disclose affiliate relationships clearly.
  • Avoid fabricated reviews, ratings and personal experiences.
  • For electrical equipment, emphasize ratings and safe use.
