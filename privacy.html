Privacy Policy

This page explains the basic privacy practices for CampusKit Guide.

Information we collect

CampusKit Guide does not currently require visitors to create an account or submit personal information to read the website. If a visitor voluntarily contacts us, we may receive the information included in that message.

Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like many websites, hosting infrastructure may process technical information needed to deliver pages securely, such as IP address, browser information and request data. We do not operate our own advertising tracker or analytics system on this site at this time.

Affiliate links

Some pages may contain links to third-party retailers or affiliate destinations. Those websites may collect information according to their own privacy policies when you visit them.

Cookies

CampusKit Guide does not intentionally set its own advertising or tracking cookies at this time. Third-party services linked from the site may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

Third-party websites

When you leave CampusKit Guide through a link, the destination website's privacy policy applies. Review that policy before providing personal information or making a purchase.

Changes

This policy may be updated if we add services such as analytics, contact forms, newsletters or other features.
